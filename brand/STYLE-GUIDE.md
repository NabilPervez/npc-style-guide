# 1. Brand Story

## Why
Brilliant people get held back by brand and tech work they shouldn't have to carry. NPC exists to carry it for them: honestly, and done right.

## Who we are
Nabil Pervez Consulting (NPC) is a Dallas–Fort Worth studio for brand, web, AI and product. You get a big-studio creative exec on your team at a small-studio price: someone who has built for LEGO, Riot Games, Coca-Cola and Red Bull, now working inside growing companies.

## Vision
Every ambitious business gets the craft and the AI that used to belong only to companies with big-studio budgets.

## Mission
We remove the creative and technical friction that holds ambitious people back. We scope it honestly, price it once and build it right, so our clients can be excellent at what they actually do.

## Behind the name
**NPC** is Nabil Pervez Consulting. The initials are the brand; the founder is the guarantee. In games, an NPC is the character who's always there to help the player move forward. That's the job.
**The N mark ("Nebtune")** is a gold N drawn from the same geometry as the circle lattice: one letter, many connected circles. Many disciplines, one system.

## Origin
Nabil spent 10+ years on the other side of the table: marketing projects for esports and entertainment (PVP Live, Infinite Esports), then co-founding and running AOE Creative, a multi-million-dollar award-winning agency serving LEGO, Red Bull, Coca-Cola, Riot Games and Activision Blizzard, with a $5M+ portfolio and a 90% referral rate. Then he went inside enterprise to implement AI at scale.
He saw the same thing everywhere: growing companies couldn't afford the senior people big brands had, and agencies sold them hours instead of outcomes. In January 2024 he started NPC to give those companies the same rigor with none of the overhead. AI-assisted building makes it possible to deliver studio-grade work at a fraction of the agency cost.

## Motto
**Built on honesty. Driven by excellence.**

## Promise line
**Done right.**

## Manifesto
We tell you the truth about scope and price, before you sign.
We'd rather say no than sell you something you don't need.
We show proof, not adjectives.
We do the work once, and we do it right.
We move fast, and we never break your trust.
We carry the weight so you can do your best work.
Then we hand you the keys.

## Values, privately held
NPC's standards come from two principles Nabil lives by: **Ihsan**, doing things with excellence and beauty as if someone is always watching, and **Amanah**, trustworthiness with what's been placed in your care. Public copy never names them; it shows them through behaviour. *(Decision D-04.)*

# 2. Brand Strategy

## Why recap
Why choose NPC over doing nothing, hiring in-house or a big agency? Because you get senior, studio-grade brand, web, AI and product leadership, honestly scoped and fixed-priced, for less than one full-time hire, and you don't have to manage it.

## Audiences
| | **Primary: The Growing Operator** | **Secondary: The Founder Launching** | **Tertiary: The Agency Partner** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who | Founder, CEO or head of marketing at a mid-size business (≈10–200 people) | Funded startup or indie studio founder (games, tech, consumer) | Agency or studio owner who needs senior delivery capacity |
| Age | 32–55 | 28–45 | 30–50 |
| Trigger | Brand looks smaller than the company; site doesn't convert; team is drowning in manual work; AI pressure from the board | Launch, raise, Kickstarter or rebrand with a hard date | Over capacity, or a client needs AI/product skills they don't have |

**Not a fit:** businesses shopping for the cheapest logo; anyone who wants hours billed rather than outcomes; requests that conflict with NPC's values; companies wanting T-Mobile-adjacent work while Nabil is employed there.

## Positioning statement
For **growing companies who need senior brand, web, AI and product help but can't justify a big agency or a full-time exec**, NPC is the **fractional digital and AI partner** that **puts a big-studio creative exec on your team, scoped honestly and priced once**, unlike **agencies that bill hours and juniors, or freelancers you have to manage**.

**Category to own:** Fractional Digital & AI Partner.

## Competitive landscape
| Alternative | Strength | Shortfall NPC exploits |
|---|---|---|
| Full-service agency | Bench depth, polish | Expensive, slow, juniors do the work, hours not outcomes |
| Freelancers / marketplaces | Cheap, fast for one task | No strategy, you manage them, inconsistent quality |

**Map** (x = seniority, y = builds it end to end): agencies sit high on building, mid on seniority, high on price; freelancers are low on both; fractional CMOs are senior but don't build. **NPC sits in the open corner: senior and builds it, at a growing-company price.**

**Primary: The Sage.** Proven, analytical, the guide who's done it before. Behaviour: shows proof, gives a straight answer on scope and price, teaches clients AI-first workflows.

**Never:** the Outlaw or the Jester. No stunts, no hype, no jokes in sales copy.

## Authentic voice
**Straight.** We say what it costs, what it takes and what we think, before you have to ask. Honesty is the first value, so the voice never hedges or upsells.
- Sounds like: "This takes three weeks and costs one fixed price. Half to start."
- Doesn't sound like: "Pricing varies depending on a range of factors. Let's hop on a call!"

**Proven.** We earn trust with numbers and names, not adjectives.
- Sounds like: "Built for LEGO, Riot and Coca-Cola."
- Doesn't sound like: "World-class, award-winning, best-in-class solutions."

**Calm.** Short sentences. Periods, not exclamation marks. The voice lowers the client's heart rate.
- Sounds like: "We've got it. You'll see a draft Thursday."
- Doesn't sound like: "Don't worry!!! We're on it 🚀"

**For you.** Every line is about the reader's outcome, in second person.
- Sounds like: "You focus on your business. We build the brand and the tech."
- Doesn't sound like: "NPC leverages proprietary methodologies to deliver value."

## Writing mechanics
- **Person:** "you" for the reader. "We" for NPC the firm (site, proposals). "I" when Nabil speaks personally (LinkedIn, about, email, outreach).
- **Headlines:** 3–6 words, sentence case, end with a period, two-tone (white statement + grey continuation).
- **Labels:** UPPERCASE, 13px, 0.14em tracking, champagne.
- **Punctuation:** periods over exclamation marks (zero in sales copy); Oxford comma; en dash for ranges (10–200); middle dot (·) as separator; no ellipses in UI.
- **Numbers:** numerals always; "40+", "10M+", "$5M+", "30%"; spell out nothing above nine in body copy; units close (48 hours, 20-minute call).
- **Dates:** 23 Oct 2026 in docs; "Thursday" in client updates.
- **Emoji:** never in brand, site or proposals.
- **Links & CTAs:** verb-first, specific: "Book a 20-minute call", "See the work". Never "Click here" or "Learn more".
- **Names:** clients are typeset, never shown as logos without licensed art. T-Mobile never appears on NPC pages.

## Inclusive language
- Write for any founder: avoid "guys", gendered defaults and sports-bro metaphors.
- Plain English first; explain any acronym on first use (AEM, RAG).
- Never use disability as metaphor ("crazy", "blind spot" → "gap").
- Use real names and titles the way people give them.
- Alt text describes what's shown and whose work it is.

## 8. Visual language
### The circle lattice
Interlocking circles, champagne #AB9563 stroke 1.3px, **88px tile** (56px in the hero on phones, 80px desktop). It's the only ornament. It means "many disciplines, one system." It is animated only in the hero and closing band; elsewhere it sits faint (12–28%) and masked toward content.

### Imagery
- **Work:** real portfolio pieces as photo tiles (14px radius) in the bento reel, with frosted caption pills naming client + offering.
- **Portrait:** clean, editorial, dignified portrait of Nabil next to a statement. Natural light, plain dark ground, no props.
- **Line drawings:** literal and spare: hairline strokes, one gold accent, drawn on when revealed (step cards, trophy).
- **Never:** stock handshakes, glowing AI brains, robots, neon, lens flare, or another brand's imagery.

### Iconography (new rule, D-09)
Hairline icons (1.5px stroke, round caps), `currentColor`, sizes 13–14 (buttons), 18 (contact rows), 24 (cards). Glyphs in use: gold ★ on awards, — as bullet marker, ↗ on outbound links. No emoji.

### Motion
| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Easing | `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)` |
| Reveal | opacity 0→1 + 30px rise, 900ms, 140ms stagger |
| Hover | colour/opacity 400–500ms |
| Press | scale(.97) |
| Hero rings | two rings ripple outward over 12s, offset half a cycle |
| Gold N | breathes (brightness 1→1.18) every 6s |
| Gold text | shimmer, 6s linear loop |
| Loops | client names, work reel, testimonials drift slowly; pause on hover, focus, touch; pause button |
| Reduced motion | everything stops; loops become one static set; content visible without JS |
| Retired | hover lift, shine sweep, sibling dimming |

### Nebtune motion backgrounds (video, streams, presentations)
Four 1920×1080 looping backgrounds built for screens: ground #0D1522, the champagne lattice at 56px muted to 13%, a brightened gold lattice revealed through an animated mask, and a light noise layer.
| Name | Motion | Default loop | Controls |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wave** | A diagonal band of gold sweeps corner to corner | 10s | speed, intensity |
| **Ripple** | Rings of gold expand from the centre | 12s | speed, intensity, rings 1–4 |
| **Tide** | A horizontal band rises bottom to top while breathing | 14s (breathe 7s) | speed, intensity |
| **Constellation** | Points of the lattice flicker gold at random | 7–13s each | speed, intensity, density 6–60 |
**Rule:** grain is allowed *only* in these broadcast backgrounds; web pages stay grain-free (D-10).
