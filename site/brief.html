Creative brief. The why first.

Six phases from the Creative Brief V4. Every answer carries its reason.

C = confirmed by Nabil or shipped · I = inferred from his sources, needs sign-off · O = open.

v1.0 · 23 Oct 2026 · Status: answered on Nabil's behalf, awaiting his sign-off

Sources: NPC Brand Kickoff Questionnaire (29 Mar 2026), resume (github.com/NabilPervez/resume), npc-design-system (2026 direction), npc-homepage-redesign (live), portfolio images, past style guides in Drive, Notion Second Brain change log. Status key: C = Confirmed (Nabil said or shipped it) · I = Inferred from sources, needs sign-off · O = Open

Snapshot
Why Ambitious people are held back by brand and tech friction they shouldn't have to carry. NPC carries it, honestly and done right, so they can be excellent at their own work. (I)
Pitch A big-studio creative exec on your team, at a small-studio price. Brand, web, AI and product, run by someone who built for LEGO, Riot and Coca-Cola. (C, homepage)
Primary audience Founders and marketing leads at growing mid-size businesses (roughly 10–200 people) who need senior creative and AI help but can't justify a big agency or a full-time exec. (C: target client is mid-size on monthly retainer)
Catalyst NPC moved from "branding + web" (March kickoff) to four offerings and an AI-forward retainer. The 2026 homepage and design system shipped 23 Oct 2026. The written strategy hadn't caught up. (C)
Scope Full style guide: strategy, pyramid, voice, visual identity reported from the design system, digital guide. (C)
Decider Nabil Pervez, single point of contact, final veto. (C)

Phase 1: Core discovery

Question Choice Why
Archetype Sage (primary) + Caregiver / Servant Leader (secondary) 10+ years and named enterprise clients make NPC the proven guide; the motive is service: carrying the client's burden. C
Interaction tone Highly structured expert who is warm one-on-one Clients come stressed and overloaded; they want a clear plan from someone who has done it, delivered kindly. I
Core theme visual Internal / abstract: connecting the dots, geometric harmony The circle lattice is literally interlocking circles: many disciplines, one system. Also echoes Islamic geometric art without being a cliché. C
Sophistication (1 corporate → 10 minimalist) 7 Premium executive consultancy, expressed through minimal, modern restraint. C
Cultural cues Subtle / implicit Values of honesty, modesty and excellence are felt in behaviour and geometry, never shown as symbols. C
Dual themes Structured leads; soft details (circles, slow easing) Structure = trust and predictable delivery; circles and fades keep it human. C
Primary hook "This feels trustworthy and safe" + "This looks premium" Target emotion is relief: "finally, someone I can trust to handle this perfectly." C
Visual anti-goals Hustle-culture agency flash, neon gradients, stock "AI brain" imagery, drop shadows, juvenile/playful, dusty corporate Each says "loud and cheap" or "old and slow", the two things NPC is not. C
Visual success metric Long-term trust and recognition, then conversion (booked calls) Retainer business lives on trust and referrals (90% referral rate history). I
Redesign catalyst Proactive: new offer (Fractional Digital & AI Partner), new homepage Lead generation is being built from zero; the brand has to sell without Nabil in the room. C
Evolution ↔ revolution 4: evolution Keep the N mark, gold, lattice and ink; retire the serif, shadows, grain and hover lift. C
Equity to preserve Selective: gold N (Nebtune) mark, champagne circle lattice, ink ground, NPC monogram, business card These are on the printed card and every past asset; they're the recognisable parts. C
Adjectives to shed Freelancer, gamer-agency, one-man-band, decorative Past work skews esports; NPC now sells to all growing businesses. I
Adjectives to adopt Senior, calm, trustworthy, exact, modern Mirror the Sage + Servant Leader archetype and the "relief" emotion. I
Trust metrics Booked calls from the site, proposal win rate, retainer renewal rate, referral rate Retainer economics depend on renewal; referrals have historically driven the business. I
Competitor & verbal anti-goals Don't sound like a growth-hack agency ("10x", "crush it", "synergy", "cutting-edge AI solutions"); never borrow another brand's names, colours or copy Proximity to hype erodes the honesty promise. C

Phase 2: Architecture

Question Choice Why
Structure Branded house: NPC is the one brand; offerings are descriptors One person, one standard; splitting into sub-brands would dilute a small firm. I
Sub-brand elasticity Moderate: offerings get their own colour family only Colour helps buyers navigate four services without new logos. C
Equity flow Bottom-up / founder-led: Nabil's track record lifts NPC Clients buy Nabil's experience (LEGO, Riot, Coca-Cola, Red Bull). I
Future scaling Full design system with tokenized components Already built (npc-design-system), reused across homepage, cards and decks. C
Navigation Clear escalation: one project → retainer Two buy cards; retainer is the recommended (gold) option. C

Phase 3: Verbal identity

Question Choice Why
Dinner-party persona Quiet authority: speaks rarely, always with weight; warm once you're talking Sage + Servant Leader; "quiet confidence" is in the kickoff. C
Formality (1 formal → 10 chatty) 4 Plain and direct, never stiff; no jokes in sales copy. I
Complexity (1 technical → 10 simple) 8 Buyers are smart but not technical; clarity is the service. I
Emotional journey Top: "This person is serious." Mid: "I know exactly what I'll get and what it costs." Post: "That weight is gone." Relief and peace of mind are the conversion levers. C
Tone flex Celebratory: one proud fact, no exclamation pile-ups. Crisis: own it first, give the fix and the date. Honesty is the first value; it shows most when things go wrong. I
Vocabulary DO: plain verbs, numbers, named clients, "you". DON'T: buzzwords, emoji, superlatives without proof, T-Mobile on NPC pages. Rules learned from the 2026 review. C
Education level College-educated general professional Founders and marketing leads, not engineers. I

Phase 4: Visual definition (as built)

Question Choice Why
Core colour Neutrality & timelessness (ink #07101F) with warmth (one gold gradient) Executive presence + premium accent; navy-black reads deeper than pure black. C
Supporting palette Bold & contrasting: ink with a single gold accent; offering colours for wayfinding only Separates signal from noise. C
Imagery Product/craft-first portfolio tiles + editorial portrait of Nabil Show proof, don't explain it; the personal connection sells a founder-led firm. C
Geometry Hybrid: structured grid exterior, soft circle lattice and 20px radii inside Kickoff asked for sharp; the shipped system settled on circles. Reported as built. C
Line weight Thin & elegant: 1px hairlines, 1.3px lattice stroke Precision; shadows retired. C
Abstraction Semi-abstract: the gold N (Nebtune) mark + NPC monogram Initials-led per kickoff; the N is the icon at small sizes. C
Symmetry Symmetrical marks, asymmetric layouts Marks feel stable; pages feel modern. C
Case Brand name as NPC; headlines sentence case; labels UPPERCASE Uppercase reserved for authority moments. C
Type personality Sans-serif: Outfit only One face; hierarchy comes from colour and size. C
Container Open, with the N tile (8px radius) as the only contained version Floats in whitespace on digital. C
Texture Flat: hairlines, no shadows, no grain on web pages 2026 rule. Grain survives only inside the Nebtune motion backgrounds. C
Icon–text Equal partnership in the lockup; N mark alone at small sizes Kickoff: wordmark hero, but needs an easy icon. C
Negative space Open only if organic Not a deal breaker. C
Era Timeless contemporary The 10-year mark. C
Pacing Slow & deliberate cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), 900ms reveals: noticed, never showy. C
Native environments Screen-first digital (web, social, video), print business card, decks Where buyers meet NPC. C

Phase 5: Responsive logo

Question Choice Why
Extremes Favicon 16px, social avatar, nav N tile, email signature, business card, video intro, slide masters Small digital is the hardest; it must survive as the N alone. C
Subtraction Lockup → wordmark + N → N mark → N tile The N must survive at every scale. C
Constraints Reverse-out (gold on ink, gold-deep on paper); WCAG AA for text Light-theme gold #9A6A10 exists for AA on paper. C

Phase 6: Governance

Question Choice Why
Veto Single point of contact: Nabil; feedback in 48 hours Matches the "review within 48 hours" promise to clients. C
Integration Design-system repo + starter PROMPT.md for AI tools; Notion Style Library; this digital guide Most NPC work is built with AI; the brand has to be machine-readable. C
Success metrics Booked calls, proposal win rate, retainer renewals, referral rate, organic search for "Nabil Pervez" Leadership (Nabil) needs pipeline, not awards. I

Contradictions resolved

  1. Kickoff "sharp & edged" vs. shipped circle lattice. The shipped system wins; geometry is reported as hybrid.
  2. Kickoff "navy blue" vs. ink #07101F. Ink is a navy-black; navy lives on as the Web offering ground #0B1C3A.
  3. Audience: SMB + premium individuals (March) vs. mid-size on retainer (now). Mid-size businesses are primary; individuals are out of scope for the retainer.
  4. Two mottos. "Built on honesty. Driven by excellence." is the motto; "Done right." is the promise/tagline. See decisions.md.

Implications downstream

  • Story: lead with service and honesty; heritage stays implicit in public copy.
  • Strategy: sell the retainer; prove with named clients and numbers.
  • Pyramid: Purpose = remove friction; Promise = done right.
  • Voice: quiet authority; numbers over adjectives.
  • Visual: report the 2026 system exactly; no new styles.
