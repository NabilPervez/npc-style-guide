09 · Identity

Imagery and icons. Show proof.

Real work, a real portrait, spare line drawings. Nothing stock.

Work tiles.

Real portfolio pieces, 14px radius, a frosted caption pill naming the client and the offering.

Past work is credited to the agency where it was made (AOE Creative) in case studies.

Portrait.

Now I build yours. Same rigor, none of the overhead.

  • Clean, editorial, dignified
  • Plain dark ground, natural light
  • Always paired with a statement
  • No props, no crossed arms, no stock office

Line drawings and icons.

Literal and spare: 1.5px hairlines, round caps, one gold accent. Drawn on when revealed.

01 · Talk.

02 · Plan.

03 · Launch.

Glyphs in use: gold ★ on awards, — as the bullet marker, ↗ on outbound links. Icons are currentColor at 13–14 (buttons), 18 (contact rows), 24 (cards). No emoji.

Never.

  • Stock handshakes and office smiles
  • Glowing AI brains, robots, circuit boards
  • Neon, lens flare, chrome
  • Client logos without licensed art
  • Another brand's imagery
