Authentic voice

Straight. We say what it costs, what it takes and what we think, before you have to ask. Honesty is the first value, so the voice never hedges or upsells. - Sounds like: "This takes three weeks and costs one fixed price. Half to start." - Doesn't sound like: "Pricing varies depending on a range of factors. Let's hop on a call!"

Proven. We earn trust with numbers and names, not adjectives. - Sounds like: "Built for LEGO, Riot and Coca-Cola." - Doesn't sound like: "World-class, award-winning, best-in-class solutions."

Calm. Short sentences. Periods, not exclamation marks. The voice lowers the client's heart rate. - Sounds like: "We've got it. You'll see a draft Thursday." - Doesn't sound like: "Don't worry!!! We're on it 🚀"

For you. Every line is about the reader's outcome, in second person. - Sounds like: "You focus on your business. We build the brand and the tech." - Doesn't sound like: "NPC leverages proprietary methodologies to deliver value."

Messaging house

10 seconds: NPC puts a big-studio creative exec on your team, at a small-studio price: brand, web, AI and product, done right. 30 seconds: I'm Nabil. I spent ten years building for LEGO, Riot, Coca-Cola and Red Bull. Now I do the same work for growing companies: brand, website, AI automations and product, run as one team. You get one scope, one fixed price and a senior person doing the work. Most clients start with one project and stay on retainer. 2 minutes: Add the problem (agencies bill hours and hand you juniors; freelancers need managing; a full-time hire costs six figures and covers one discipline), the proof (40+ brands, Tempest Award, 30% engagement lift for Riot, 2.2M Coca-Cola reach at half the CPM, 90% referral rate), the process (Talk. Plan. Launch.) and the ask (book a 20-minute call). Boilerplate (50 words): Nabil Pervez Consulting (NPC) is a Dallas–Fort Worth studio for brand, web, AI and product. Founded by Nabil Pervez, who has built for LEGO, Riot Games, Coca-Cola and Red Bull, NPC gives growing companies a big-studio creative exec at a small-studio price, with fixed scopes and senior hands on every project.

Headline formulas 1. Noun. Verb the outcome. "Brand. Look the part." 2. Statement. Grey continuation. "Everything starts | with a call." 3. Promise in three words. "Done right." / "Ship it."

Writing mechanics

  • Person: "you" for the reader. "We" for NPC the firm (site, proposals). "I" when Nabil speaks personally (LinkedIn, about, email, outreach).
  • Headlines: 3–6 words, sentence case, end with a period, two-tone (white statement + grey continuation).
  • Labels: UPPERCASE, 13px, 0.14em tracking, champagne.
  • Punctuation: periods over exclamation marks (zero in sales copy); Oxford comma; en dash for ranges (10–200); middle dot (·) as separator; no ellipses in UI.
  • Numbers: numerals always; "40+", "10M+", "$5M+", "30%"; spell out nothing above nine in body copy; units close (48 hours, 20-minute call).
  • Dates: 23 Oct 2026 in docs; "Thursday" in client updates.
  • Emoji: never in brand, site or proposals.
  • Links & CTAs: verb-first, specific: "Book a 20-minute call", "See the work". Never "Click here" or "Learn more".
  • Names: clients are typeset, never shown as logos without licensed art. T-Mobile never appears on NPC pages.

Inclusive language

  • Write for any founder: avoid "guys", gendered defaults and sports-bro metaphors.
  • Plain English first; explain any acronym on first use (AEM, RAG).
  • Never use disability as metaphor ("crazy", "blind spot" → "gap").
  • Use real names and titles the way people give them.
  • Alt text describes what's shown and whose work it is.
