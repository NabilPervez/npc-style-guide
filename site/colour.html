The gold gradient: one per view

linear-gradient(120deg, #F5D97A 0%, #DDB84A 40%, #C88B20 75%, #E8B840 100%) Use it for one thing per view: the highlighted word, the primary button, or the recommended card. Ink text on gold = 10:1.

Light theme (print and documents)

Paper #EEF2FA / #F8FBFF, ink text, gold-deep #9A6A10 for accents. Finding: #9A6A10 on #EEF2FA measures 4.2:1, AA for large text only. Use it for headlines and marks on paper, not body text (D-08).

Print

Business card (from the PDF): ink #131921, panel #2C2E35, champagne #AC9760. 3.5×2in, 3.75×2.25in with bleed.

Usage ratio

~85% ink grounds · ~12% text and hairlines · ≤3% gold. Offering colours only inside their own tile, pill or dot.
