Purpose

Remove the creative and technical friction that holds ambitious people back. - Serve growing companies that deserve big-studio quality without big-studio cost - Turn brand, web, AI and product from burdens into advantages - Free clients to be excellent at their own craft - Build long-term partnerships, not one-off invoices

Values

Value Behaviour A decision it guided
Truth first We give a straight answer on scope and price before anyone signs. Fixed price, one document, no hidden hourly overage.
Excellence (Ihsan) We do it as if someone is always watching, because the details are the work. Every page checked at 375px and 1280px before it ships.
Trust (Amanah) What you hand us (your brand, data, customers) is protected like our own. Client logos never shown without licensed art; no T-Mobile on NPC pages.
Service We carry the weight, so you don't have to. 48-hour review cycles; we chase, you approve.
Calm We move fast and never break things. Slow easing, one accent, no showy motion in the brand itself.
Proof Every claim carries a number or a named client. No testimonial or stat appears unless it's real.

Pillars

  • Honesty. Straight scope, straight price, straight feedback.
  • Craft. Big-studio standards, every pixel and every line of code.
  • Momentum. Visible progress every week; it goes live.

Functional traits: what NPC does differently

  • Senior hands, not juniors. The person you meet is the person doing the work.
  • Fixed price, one document. The SOW is the proposal. Half to start.
  • Four in one. Brand, web, AI and product under one roof and one standard.
  • AI-native delivery. AI-assisted research, prototyping and building compress timelines and cost.
  • Enterprise-proven. Built for LEGO, Riot, Coca-Cola, Red Bull; implements AI inside the enterprise.
  • Systems, not one-offs. Every brand ships with a design system and AI-ready prompt.

Perception: "After working with NPC, I feel…"

Relieved - Someone finally owns this - I know what it costs and when it's done - I stopped worrying about the tech

Capable - My team does more with less (AI) - I understand what was built and why - I can run it after handover

Proud - We look like the company we're becoming - I send people to our site without apologising - Investors and customers take us seriously

Persona

The calm senior in the room. The one who's seen it before, tells you the truth kindly, and quietly gets it done. STEADY. HONEST. EXACT.

Promise

Done right. Honest scope, one fixed price, senior craft, and work that goes live: every time.
